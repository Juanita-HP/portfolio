Databricks AI Agent Hackathon
27 MARCH 2026
⚡ Participant
Organized by: EY Montréal & Databricks

During this challenge, our team developed an AI agent designed to help companies identify in less than 30 seconds the optimal building location based on their clients needs. By leveraging data and intelligent analysis, our solution evaluates key factors such as parking availability, foot traffic, street context, and accessibility to support smarter, data-driven decisions. Grateful to EY Montréal and Databricks for organizing such an inspiring event, and to everyone who contributed to this experience.

Shrekathon
20-22 MARCH 2026
🏆 Winner
Organized by: Concordia Game Dev Club / Concordia University

Built during a 48-hour game jam by a team of two, Jiejun and me, this project challenged us to create and launch a fully playable game from scratch in a Shrek-inspired universe. Our game, Shrek: 7 Layers Onion, went on to win Best Themed Game, reflecting both creative execution and strong technical problem-solving under pressure. Throughout the jam, we designed different scenarios and gameplay moments that matched the theme while keeping the experience fun and coherent. No AI was used in the creation process. Link: https://www.roblox.com/games/119841885626750/Shrek-7-Layers-Onion
