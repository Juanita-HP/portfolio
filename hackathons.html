Shrekathon
20-22 MARCH 2026
⚡ Participant
Organized by: Concordia Game Dev Club / Concordia University

Here is the revised version with your teammate included: Built during a 48-hour game jam by a team of two, Jiejun and me, this project challenged us to create and launch a fully playable Roblox game from scratch in a Shrek-inspired universe. Our game, Shrek: 7 Layers Onion, went on to win Best Themed Game, reflecting both creative execution and strong technical problem-solving under pressure. Throughout the jam, we designed different scenarios and gameplay moments that matched the theme while keeping the experience fun and coherent. The project was developed using Roblox Studio and Luau, along with core game development skills applied step by step, including scripting, game logic, level design, UI implementation, playtesting, debugging, rapid prototyping, and teamwork. No AI was used in the creation process.
